Out on the web

Type an address in the bar at the top and press Enter. LexOS speaks http:// and https:// (TLS 1.3 of its own). Under QEMU the host is 10.0.2.2: run

  cd some/folder
  python3 -m http.server 8000

on it and open http://10.0.2.2:8000/. Or try https://example.com/.

Keys: arrows, PgUp/PgDn, Space - scroll; Backspace - back; Tab - the address bar; F5 - reload; Esc - quit.
