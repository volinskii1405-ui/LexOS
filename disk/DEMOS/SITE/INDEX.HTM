Out on the web

Type an address in the bar at the top and press Enter. LexOS speaks http:// and https:// (TLS 1.3 and 1.2 of its own). Words typed there instead of an address are searched for. Under QEMU the host is 10.0.2.2: run

  cd some/folder
  python3 -m http.server 8000

on it and open http://10.0.2.2:8000/. Or try https://example.com/.

Your bookmarks (Ctrl+D or the star keeps a page) and the pages you've been to.

Keys: arrows, PgUp/PgDn, Space - scroll; Backspace - back; Tab - the address bar; F5 - reload; F9 - the reader; Ctrl+I - about the page; Ctrl+U - its source; Esc - quit. Ctrl+F - find on the page (Enter, F3: the next); drag the mouse over text, then Ctrl+C copies it (Ctrl+A: all of it); Ctrl+D - bookmark; Ctrl+B - bookmarks; Ctrl+H - history. Tabs: Ctrl+T a new one, Ctrl+W closes, Ctrl+Tab the next, Ctrl+click opens a link in one. JavaScript: Ctrl+K - its console; Ctrl+J - off and on.
